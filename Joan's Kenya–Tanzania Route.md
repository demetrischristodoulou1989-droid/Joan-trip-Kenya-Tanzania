## Joining groups as a solo traveller

Shared ("joining") safaris pool solo travellers into one vehicle, which matters most in Ngorongoro where the crater vehicle fee is split between passengers. When you write to operators, ask: *"Do you have a confirmed joining departure on \[date\], how many people are booked, and what is the price if it doesn't fill?"* Check recent reviews on SafariBookings and TripAdvisor before paying a deposit.

### Kenya (Nairobi NP + Amboseli)

- **Wildebeest Eco Camp** (Langata): a backpacker base near Nairobi NP and the Giraffe Centre that runs its own shared safaris. Easiest way to meet other travellers.
- **Gracepatt Ecotours**: long-running budget operator known for joining safaris from Nairobi.
- **Bonfire Adventures**: Kenyan company with scheduled group trips, often with Kenyan travellers. Good for meeting locals rather than only backpackers.
- Ask each one specifically about a **Namanga drop on day 2**.

### Tanzania (Arusha)

- **Kilismile Trails & Safaris** (your link): day trips from Arusha, e.g. Materuni falls from $80, Chemka hot springs from $90. Ask if they pool solo travellers.
- **Kilipath African Safari**: listed on SafariBookings with shared tours (max 6 per vehicle) from Arusha.
- **SafariBookings.com**: filter tours by "shared" and "budget", start Arusha, 3 days. Compare at least 3 quotes.
- **Tanzania Cultural Tourism Programme**: community-run walks and bike tours (Mto wa Mbu, Ng'iresi, Lake Duluti). Fees go to village projects.

### All-in-one alternative

If Joan wants a group the whole way, overland companies (e.g. Absolute Africa, Intrepid, G Adventures) run Nairobi → Arusha → Zanzibar group trips of 10–20 days. Less freedom and more safari than he wants, but zero logistics. Check whether a departure fits 15 Nov.

## Before he goes: entry, health, safety

### Entry paperwork

- Passport valid 6+ months after arrival, with blank pages.
- Kenya eTA online at etakenya.go.ke, at least 2 weeks before (about $30–35).
- Tanzania e-visa (about $50 single entry for most EU passports) or visa on arrival at Namanga. The e-visa avoids queues.
- **Yellow fever certificate.** Kenya is a yellow-fever-risk country, so Tanzania can ask for proof when he crosses from Kenya. Get vaccinated before the trip and carry the card.
- Zanzibar's mandatory inbound insurance applies only if Zanzibar is the first port of entry. Joan enters Tanzania at Namanga, so it should not apply, but confirm before travel.
- Travel insurance that covers safari, hiking to \~2,700 m, and medical evacuation.

### Health

- Malaria prophylaxis: needed for Amboseli, Northern Tanzania and Zanzibar. Nairobi itself is low-risk.
- Travel clinic 6–8 weeks ahead: yellow fever, hepatitis A, typhoid, routine boosters.
- **Ebola:** UK advice (7 Oct 2026) notes an outbreak centred in the DRC and enhanced screening in Kenya, especially for arrivals from Uganda, Ethiopia and DRC. Not on his route, but expect temperature checks.
- Never drink spirits of unknown origin. FCDO warns of methanol poisoning and drink spiking in bars.

### Security, honestly

- **Nairobi:** mugging and bag-snatching are common, including by day, especially in the CBD. Use Bolt or Uber door to door at night, carry a spare phone or keep it out of sight, and stay away from protests.
- **Tanzania:** after the October 2025 election, protests and security crackdowns caused deaths. FCDO says more demonstrations are possible, often around public holidays. Avoid crowds, don't film police or protests, and don't post about politics while there. Internet has been cut before, so save key numbers offline.
- **Zanzibar:** armed robberies have occurred in Stone Town and on beaches. Avoid empty beaches after dark. Dress modestly away from the beach; fines can be $700+.
- **Roads:** avoid road travel after dark, and avoid boda-bodas.

### Weather in Nov–Dec

This is the short-rains season. Expect afternoon showers in Nairobi and Arusha, green landscapes and fewer crowds. Ngorongoro rim is cold at night (5–10 °C), so pack a fleece. Zanzibar is hot and humid with passing showers, getting drier into December. Kilimanjaro is often clearest from Amboseli at dawn.

**Kilimanjaro wildfires.** The UK Foreign Office updated its Tanzania advice on 6 Oct 2026: wildfires have closed the Rongai, Lemosho and Londorosi routes. The day hike in this plan uses the Marangu gate, which was not listed as closed, but check again in mid-November. Backup: Materuni waterfalls and a Chagga coffee walk.

**Go / no-go a week before departure:** re-read the FCDO or Danish/Spanish foreign-ministry advice for Kenya and Tanzania; confirm the Namanga drop or switch to Plan B; confirm the safari group size in writing; confirm Marangu is open.

## Travelling well

### Choose

- Community-run cultural tourism walks (Mto wa Mbu, Lake Duluti) over "Maasai village" photo stops where the fee rarely reaches residents.
- Local guides in Stone Town; the Old Slave Market memorial and Arusha Declaration Museum for history told from the region's side.
- Women's co-operatives: Kazuri Beads (Nairobi), Seaweed Centre (Paje).
- Tip safari guides and cooks directly ($10–20 per day each is common).

### Skip

- Slum "tours" in Kibera, unless run by a resident-led organisation he has checked.
- Dolphin-chasing boats at Kizimkazi, and any trip that promises swimming with wild dolphins.
- Buying safaris from street touts in Arusha or Nairobi.
- Photographing people without asking.

### How this compares with the 15-day package

The Mara Enkopang 15-day trip has about 10 safari days (Amboseli, Naivasha, Masai Mara, Serengeti, Ngorongoro, Tarangire), crosses at Isebania and ends in an all-inclusive resort. This plan keeps Amboseli and Ngorongoro, cuts safari to 5 days, adds a full week of Nairobi, a Kilimanjaro hike and 7 independent nights on Zanzibar, and crosses at Namanga instead.
